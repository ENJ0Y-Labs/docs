# Trexave Vision

- **Status:** Draft

## Vision

Build practical, reliable software that helps African businesses operate with greater clarity, control, and confidence.

## Product Principle

Solve real operational problems with understandable workflows and dependable records before adding complexity.

## Working Principles

- **Useful over flashy:** prioritize outcomes for customers over decorative complexity.
- **Reliable records:** treat sales, stock, and financial records as critical business data.
- **Clear ownership:** every initiative has an accountable owner.
- **Small, verifiable changes:** ship work that can be tested and reviewed.
- **Learn from users:** validate assumptions with real business workflows.

This is a proposed starting statement and should be reviewed by the company team before being treated as approved company policy.
