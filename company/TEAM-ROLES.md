# Team Roles

- **Status:** Draft; confirm names, authority, and responsibilities with the team.

## Product and Technical Leadership

- Own product direction, system architecture, technical standards, and final technical decisions within the agreed team process.
- Translate product goals into prioritized requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Review risks, delivery progress, and changes to scope.

## Project / Delivery Coordination

- Maintain issue status, coordinate contributors, and communicate blockers or schedule risks.
- Escalate delays and dependencies early.
- Keep decisions and action items documented.

## Client and Stakeholder Relationships

- Gather and clarify stakeholder needs.
- Communicate approved scope, progress, and feedback to the team.
- Avoid committing to unapproved product features or delivery dates.

## Contributors

- Work on agreed issues and keep changes within scope.
- Provide tests, verification steps, and clear pull-request descriptions.
- Raise blockers promptly and document relevant technical decisions.

## Ownership Rules

- Assign one directly responsible owner to every significant task.
- Record role changes and decision authority explicitly.
- This draft does not assign named people or equity and is not a substitute for an agreed team arrangement.
